How it works

Upload proof

The document's actual content is judged for legitimacy — not the filename, not a rubber stamp. Vague or unrelated documents score low, honestly.

03

A Bronze, Silver, or Gold trust credential is written on-chain, tied to your wallet — visible to any BOT Chain app from then on.
